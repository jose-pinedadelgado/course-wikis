# IS671 — Responsible AI (RAI)

> A comprehensive guide to building fair, explainable, and accountable AI systems.

This wiki covers the key concepts, techniques, and Python implementations for Responsible AI, as taught at California State University, Long Beach.

## How to Use This Wiki

Use the checkboxes above to track your progress through the material. Each chapter builds on the previous one:

1. First, understand **fairness** — what it means, how to measure it
2. Then, learn to **detect bias** in your data
3. Next, understand **why** your model makes decisions (explainability)
4. Then, learn to **fix bias** at the model and output levels
5. Finally, ensure **accountability** and **privacy** in production
