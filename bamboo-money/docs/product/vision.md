# Product Vision

## What is Bamboo Money?

Bamboo Money is a **self-hosted personal finance dashboard** designed for people who want full control over their financial data. Unlike Mint, YNAB, or Copilot, your data never leaves your machine.

## Core Principles

1. **Privacy first** — Your financial data stays on your hardware. No cloud sync, no third-party access.
2. **Zero-based budgeting** — Every dollar gets a job. Unspent budget rolls forward automatically.
3. **AI-assisted, human-controlled** — The chatbot helps you explore your data, but you make every decision.
4. **Bank-agnostic** — Import from any bank via CSV. Auto-detection handles format differences.

## Target Users

- Tech-savvy individuals who want financial visibility without giving data to fintech companies
- Couples managing household finances (multi-user support)
- Anyone frustrated with the limitations of spreadsheet-based tracking
