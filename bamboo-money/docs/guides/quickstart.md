# Quick Start

After [installation](installation.md), here's how to get started:

## 1. Log In

Navigate to `http://localhost:8400` and log in with your credentials (or `demo/demo` for the demo account).

## 2. Set Up Categories

Go to the Categories page and create your spending categories:

- Groceries, Rent, Utilities, Entertainment, Dining Out, Transportation, etc.

## 3. Import Transactions

1. Export a CSV from your bank's website
2. Go to **Import** → **Upload CSV**
3. Bamboo Money auto-detects your bank format
4. Review the preview and confirm import
5. Transactions are auto-categorized by any existing rules

## 4. Create Budgets

1. Go to **Budgets** → **New Budget**
2. Set amounts for each category
3. Watch progress bars fill as you spend

## 5. Track Net Worth

1. Go to **Net Worth** → **Add Account**
2. Enter each account with its balance and group (liquid/investment/retirement/liability/other)
3. Update balances monthly to build history
4. View metrics and charts on the net worth page

## 6. Explore with the Chatbot

Click the chat bubble in the bottom-right corner and ask:

- "What did I spend the most on this month?"
- "Show me my spending trend"
- "Am I on track with my budget?"

## 7. View Cash Flow

Navigate to **Cash Flow** to see the Sankey diagram showing how income flows through your categories.
