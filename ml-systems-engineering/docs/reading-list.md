# Reading List

## Priority 1: Must Read 🔴

### Papers
| Paper | Year | Topic | Why |
|-------|------|-------|-----|
| [Attention Is All You Need](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762) | 2017 | Transformers | The foundation — everything builds on this |
| [LoRA: Low-Rank Adaptation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.09685) | 2021 | Fine-tuning | Standard method for efficient fine-tuning |
| [InstructGPT (Ouyang et al.)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.02155) | 2022 | RLHF | Defined the SFT → RM → PPO pipeline |
| [DPO: Direct Preference Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.18290) | 2023 | Alignment | Simplified RLHF without reward model |
| [DeepSeek-R1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.12948) | 2025 | RL/Reasoning | GRPO, on-policy RL, reasoning emergence |
