# Chapter 1 — A Brief History of LLMs

## Key Concepts

AI has been "on the horizon" for 40+ years, with notable advances in chess engines, speech recognition, and self-driving cars through the 2000s–2010s.

### The Two Inflection Points

| Year | Event | Impact |
|------|-------|--------|
| **2017** | Google's "Attention Is All You Need" paper | Introduced the transformer architecture — LLMs predict next tokens |
| **2022** | ChatGPT goes viral (November) | Consumer chat interface from OpenAI brought LLMs mainstream |

### Major LLM Providers (2025)

| Provider | Model Family | Notes |
|----------|-------------|-------|
| **OpenAI** | GPT series | Founded 2015 by Sutskever, Brockman, Altman, Musk |
| **Anthropic** | Claude | Founded 2020 by ex-OpenAI researchers; popular for code and API tasks |
| **Google** | Gemini | Built by DeepMind team (acquired 2014) |
| **Meta** | Llama | Leading US open-source models |
| **Others** | Mistral, DeepSeek | Open-source alternatives (French, Chinese) |

!!! note "Key Takeaway"
    The bulk of generative AI progress happened since 2017. The field is young — fundamental architecture is only ~8 years old.
